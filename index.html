Online Play Guide

This guide explains how to play Faction Wars online. The system automatically manages the match, turns, health, gold, market, and victory conditions.

Starting the Match

  1. Choose your team: Light Faction or Dark Faction.
  2. Click join to start a new match or join an available opponent.
  3. Your name, team, health, and gold appear in the top bar.
  4. The match starts with 20 leader health and 5 gold pieces.

Match Interface

  • Top Bar: Shows health and gold of your team and opponent, and indicates whose turn it is.
  • Battlefield: Place your forces in available slots to face opponent forces.
  • Your Hand: Shows available cards for summoning, with card cost and value.
  • Bank Market: Includes cards, assistance, weapons, armor, spells, and equipment.

Cards and Resources

  • Each card has a health value shown beside or below its image.
  • Each card has a gold value or cost associated with its use in the match.
  • You cannot use a card if you don't have enough gold for its cost.
  • Card health decreases when attacked, and is removed from the battlefield when it reaches zero.

Turn Flow

  1. The system waits until your turn becomes active.
  2. Use gold to buy a card or summon a force or activate a market item.
  3. Place cards in available slots in the battlefield.
  4. Select attack target then press Attack.
  5. The system executes the attack and updates health and gold automatically, then turn passes to opponent.

Exchanging Leader Health for Gold (Emergency Funding)

  • You can sacrifice 2 health points (2❤) from your team leader to get 5 gold pieces to continue the battle.
  • This feature is available from the bank market (once per turn), or as an emergency window if you can't execute any move and don't have enough gold.
  • Your leader's health must be more than 2 to be able to sacrifice.

Victory Conditions

The system announces the result automatically when one of these conditions is met:

  1. Military Victory: Opponent leader health reaches 0.
  2. Economic Victory: Your gold reaches 25 pieces in your treasury.

Connection and Disconnection

  • Turn indicator shows when you can execute commands.
  • If disconnected, reopen the match or click back then join again.
  • Don't close the page during attack execution or purchase until match update completes.
